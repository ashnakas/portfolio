About Me

Hello! I’m Ashna Kasireddy, a Computational Media major at Georgia Tech passionate about design, usability, and human-computer interaction. My work focuses on creating thoughtful digital experiences—from redesigning LeetCode’s interface to building tools that support well-being and collaboration. I blend technical skill with design empathy to solve real-world problems.

When I’m not prototyping or coding, I’m exploring stories through visual media or working on ways to make tech more inclusive and intuitive.
