💻 LeetCode Visualizer Redesign

A redesign of LeetCode to better support component skill acquisition through five core features:

  • Progressive Learning Pathways: unlock harder problems by mastering foundational ones
  • Real-World Explanation: real-life analogies to boost conceptual learning
  • Debugger Tool: live breakpoints and variable tracing
  • Brainstorm Mode: pair-programming with other users in real time
  • AI Chatbot Help: guided hints based on your own code

This redesign centers around reducing cognitive load while building up mastery incrementally.

☀️ Space Safari

An interactive Figma prototype to teach 6–10 year-olds about the solar system. Students explore planets through animated orbits, facts, and a quiz game.

  • Taps reveal zoom-in views and planet facts
  • Quiz reinforces names, order, and fun facts
  • Includes reward stars & score tracking
  • Inspired by Duolingo ABC, Khan Academy Kids, and Solar System Scope

Backed by the segmenting principle from multimedia learning and motivational design theory.

👥 GT Buddy Finder

A tool that helps Georgia Tech students organically connect through shared classes, interests, and events.

  • Tiered Social Graph: shows 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-degree connections
  • Smart Calendar: highlights overlapping RSVPs with friends
  • Privacy Controls: choose visibility per interest or event
  • Designed to reduce social anxiety through passive discovery

Applies principles like recognition over recall, minimalist UI, and user control to foster meaningful, low-friction interaction.

📝 Notes to Self

A speculative app that lets users send emotional notes across alternate timelines to different versions of themselves. It explores:

  • Identity and self-reflection
  • Decision-making under uncertainty
  • Speculative interaction design

"Notes to Self" is a poetic, thought-provoking concept grounded in emotional computing and narrative design.
